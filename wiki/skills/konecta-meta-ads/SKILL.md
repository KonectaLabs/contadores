# Konecta Meta Ads

## Boundary

This is a marketing and creative skill. It does not operate Website Agent,
publish ads, configure campaigns, import leads or call runtime APIs.

Use the current client brief, research, approved offer and available source
assets. If any of those are missing, state the gap instead of filling it with
old runtime data.

## Creative pattern

Lead with one problem the buyer recognizes immediately. Show the desired
outcome and remove a concrete objection. Keep the visual understandable before
the caption is read.

Use this hierarchy:

1. one short hook;
2. one clear problem or outcome;
3. one visual proof or mechanism;
4. a small trust cue such as the professional category;
5. a compact action cue, usually WhatsApp.

Do not make the client's portrait, firm name or logo the main idea unless the
campaign explicitly depends on personal authority. Do not invent reviews,
results, credentials, dashboards or customer evidence.

## Copy formula

Prefer outcome-first copy:

```text
Get <desired result> without <cost, delay, risk, or hated mechanism>.
```

Support it with one mechanism and one next action. Avoid vague automation
claims, feature lists and guaranteed outcomes.

## Image prompt

Specify:

- platform and aspect ratio;
- target buyer and market;
- single problem/outcome;
- scene, subject and focal point;
- exact on-image text;
- visual style, palette and contrast;
- small trust/action cue;
- prohibited elements and false evidence.

Generate three genuinely different concepts when testing angles. Change the
idea, not only the palette.

## Sources and storage

Prefer meeting transcripts, client briefs, current page copy, approved photos,
logos and current market research. Store approved campaign artifacts under a
clear client/campaign folder in `media/ads/` with source notes and final copy.

## Review

Confirm that the ad has one buyer, one promise, one dominant visual and one
action. Verify legibility at feed size, safe claims, source-backed facts and a
clear difference between test variants.
